Reliable Dryer Vent Repair Solutions

A damaged dryer vent is more than an inconvenience — it's a safety hazard. Crushed, disconnected, or leaking vents trap heat and moisture inside your walls, increase fire risk, and force your dryer to work harder with every cycle.


HD Pro Cleaning's technicians diagnose and repair all types of dryer vent damage quickly and thoroughly. Whether it's a crushed section of duct, a disconnected joint, or a damaged exterior cap, we restore safe, efficient venting.

Our Dryer Vent Repair Work

Crushed Duct Repair

Crushed or kinked dryer duct sections are replaced to restore full, unrestricted airflow.

Disconnected Joint Repair

Separated duct connections are rejoined, secured, and sealed against air and lint leakage.

Leaking Duct Sealing

Leaking joints and seams are sealed to prevent moisture and lint from escaping into wall cavities.

Code Compliance Repair

Non-compliant vent materials (plastic or foil) are replaced with metal duct to meet safety codes.

Commercial Vent Repair

Dryer vent repairs for laundromats, apartment buildings, and commercial laundry facilities.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know my dryer vent needs repair?

Signs include clothes not drying, the dryer overheating, a burning smell, or visible damage to vent sections.

Can you repair plastic or foil dryer vents?

We replace them. Plastic and foil vents are fire hazards — we upgrade to code-compliant metal duct.

How long does a repair take?

Most repairs are completed in 1 to 2 hours. Complex repairs involving wall access may take longer.

Do you repair commercial dryer vents?

Yes. We service laundromats, apartment laundry rooms, and commercial laundry facilities.
