Expert Air Duct Cleaning Solutions

Your home's air duct system circulates air through every room — but over time, dust, pet dander, pollen, mold spores, and other contaminants accumulate inside the ducts. Every time your HVAC system runs, these particles are pushed back into the air your family breathes. The result is poor indoor air quality, allergy symptoms, and a system that works harder than it should.


At HD Pro Cleaning, our NADCA-compliant cleaning process removes built-up contaminants from your entire duct system — not just the vents you can see. We use professional-grade equipment and proven techniques to restore clean, healthy airflow for homes and businesses across New York.

Signs Your Ducts Need Cleaning

Excessive Dust

Dust settles on surfaces quickly after cleaning — a sign that your ducts are circulating contaminated air throughout the home.

Allergy or Asthma Symptoms

Increased sneezing, coughing, or breathing difficulties indoors often point to airborne allergens originating from dirty ducts.

Musty or Stale Odors

Persistent odors from vents indicate mold, mildew, or bacterial buildup inside the duct system that cleaning will eliminate.

Weak or Uneven Airflow

Rooms that never reach the right temperature may have blocked or heavily contaminated ducts restricting airflow.

Visible Mold or Debris at Vents

Dark residue, mold growth, or visible debris around vent openings is a clear sign the duct system needs immediate attention.

Recent Renovation or Construction

Construction dust and debris settle inside ducts during remodels — cleaning removes particles before they circulate through your home.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should air ducts be cleaned?

The NADCA recommends cleaning every 3 to 5 years. Homes with pets, allergies, or recent renovations may benefit from more frequent cleaning.

How long does duct cleaning take?

Most residential duct cleaning services take 2 to 4 hours depending on the size of the home and the system.

Will it make a mess in my home?

No. Our negative pressure system contains all debris inside the duct system. We protect your home and leave no mess behind.

Is duct cleaning safe for children and pets?
