Dryer Vent Rerouting for Safety & Efficiency

Many dryer vent systems were installed with improper routing — too many bends, excessive length, or termination points that don't meet code. These issues restrict airflow, increase lint buildup, and dramatically raise the risk of dryer fires.


HD Pro Cleaning reroutes dryer vent systems to optimize the path from dryer to exterior exhaust. We shorten run lengths, eliminate unnecessary bends, and ensure proper exterior termination for safe, efficient venting.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my dryer vent needs rerouting?

Signs include very long drying times, excessive heat, frequent blockages, or a vent run with many bends.

What is the maximum dryer vent length?

Most codes allow up to 25 feet of rigid duct, reduced by 5 feet for each 90-degree bend.

Will rerouting require opening walls or ceilings?

Sometimes, depending on the new route. We discuss all access requirements and options before starting.

How long does rerouting take?

Simple reroutes can be done in a few hours. Complex projects involving wall access may take a full day.
