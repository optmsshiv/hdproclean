Our HVAC Cleaning Services

Evaporator Coil Cleaning

Dirty evaporator coils reduce cooling efficiency and cause freezing. We clean them thoroughly to restore proper heat exchange and airflow.

Blower Wheel & Fan Cleaning

Buildup on blower wheels restricts airflow and creates noise. We remove all debris for quiet, powerful air circulation throughout your system.

Drain Pan & Line Cleaning

Clogged drain pans cause water damage and mold growth. We flush and clean drain pans and condensate lines to prevent costly moisture issues.

Benefits of Professional HVAC Cleaning

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should HVAC systems be cleaned?

Most residential HVAC systems benefit from a professional cleaning every 1 to 2 years. Commercial systems with higher usage may require annual or more frequent service.

What are signs my HVAC needs cleaning?

High energy bills, uneven heating or cooling, musty odors, excessive dust, or the system running longer than usual are all signs of a dirty HVAC system.

How long does HVAC cleaning take?

A full residential HVAC cleaning typically takes 2 to 4 hours. Commercial systems vary depending on the number of units and accessibility.

Do you clean both heating and cooling components?

Yes. We clean the complete system — evaporator and condenser coils, blower wheels, air handlers, drain pans, and all accessible internal components.

Is HVAC cleaning the same as duct cleaning?

They are related but different. Duct cleaning focuses on the duct system. HVAC cleaning covers the mechanical components — coils, fans, handlers, and drain systems. We offer both.

Can I stay home during HVAC cleaning?

Yes. Our technicians work cleanly and efficiently with minimal disruption to your routine. Most homeowners stay home throughout the service.
