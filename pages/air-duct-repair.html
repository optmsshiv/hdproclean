Expert Air Duct Repair Solutions

Damaged or leaking air ducts can cause your HVAC system to work overtime, driving up energy bills and reducing comfort throughout your home or business. Even small cracks, holes, or disconnected joints can lead to significant air loss.


At HD Pro Cleaning, our certified technicians diagnose and repair all types of duct damage quickly and efficiently. We restore your system to peak performance using professional-grade materials and proven repair techniques.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my ducts need repair?

Signs include high energy bills, uneven temperatures, unusual noises, or visible damage to ductwork.

How long does duct repair take?

Most residential repairs are completed in a single visit, typically 2 to 4 hours.

Do you repair both flexible and rigid ducts?

Yes. We repair all duct types including flexible, sheet metal, and fiberboard systems.

Will you need to open walls or ceilings?

Most repairs are done through access panels or existing openings — wall access is rare and always discussed beforehand.
